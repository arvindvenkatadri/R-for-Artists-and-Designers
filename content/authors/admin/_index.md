This course will give Art and Design students a foundation in the principles and practice of data visualization using R.

The viewpoint of this course is of using R to **create geometric metaphors**. Diverse domains have their own uniquely structured data. These will be depicted using geometric metaphors to enable better understanding of the underlying circumstances and realities.


> NOTE:
>The content here is good for beginners who want to self-study (and self-code) to pick basics of Data Viz in R. This website was created in 2021 and a great deal has chenged in the R world since then. 

>For a more structured, and comprehensive Course on Exploratory Data Visualization and Foundations of Statistics in R, please head to <u><https://madhatterguide.netlify.app></u>. All practices that are current in the R world, along with new content will be on this website. 

>The current website [R For Artists](https://r-fir-artists.netlify.app) will remain as is.
